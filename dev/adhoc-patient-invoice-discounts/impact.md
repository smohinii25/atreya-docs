# Discounts on patient invoices before payment: Impact analysis

> Generated by `/atreya:impact` from `impact-facts.json` (scripts collect the facts; Claude writes the analysis). Status **Draft** until a human reviews it. Evidence classes: **Verified** (code at the stamped commit, `Repo:path:line`), **Inferred**, **Assumption**, **Unknown**, **Not affected**.

## 1. Change

- **Source**: free text
- **Ticket**: `adhoc-patient-invoice-discounts`

> Allow authorised users to apply discounts to patient invoices before collecting payment. The final payable amount must be recalculated and reflected in payment records, patient billing history and revenue reports.

**What is asked**: Adding a discount changes how a visit's fee is stored and how the amount owed and earned is worked out, and that number is read by billing history, the patient portal, the dashboard and every generated report.

### Requirement understanding

**Current behaviour**
- There is no patient invoice in the code. A patient's fee for one visit is stored as two numbers on that visit's row in the medical_report table: fee_charged and fee_paid. [Verified: `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Models/MedicalReport.php:17,41-42`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/PatientDetailsController.php:2959-2960`]
- Consultation fees and therapy fees are kept separately inside the visit's recommendations JSON, not in their own columns. [Verified: `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/PatientDetailsController.php:2985-2992`]
- The doctor types fee charged, fee paid, consultation fees and therapy fees as four separate whole numbers (digits only, at most 5 characters). Nothing calculates one from another, and nothing checks that fee paid is not more than fee charged. [Verified: `Nadi-Tarangini-Frontend:resources/views/live_nadi/modal/fees/fee_details.blade.php:70-85`, `Nadi-Tarangini-Frontend:public/js/live_nadi/feeDetails.js:619-633`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/PatientDetailsController.php:2475-2478`]

[Verified: `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/PatientDetailsController.php:2428-2436`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/Client/EditProfileController.php:275-285`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/AuthController.php:1222-1230`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Http/Controllers/AuthController.php:2080`, `Ashoka-World-Backend:app/Services/GenerateAarogyaDarshika.php:4313-4334`]

**Proposed behaviour**
- Store the discount as a new whole-number amount per visit on medical_report. fee_charged keeps its meaning (price before discount). Payable = fee_charged - discount; still owed = payable - fee_paid. [Assumption, A4]
- The patient's doctor may always give a discount; any other user only when the NT admin grants a new "Give discount" feature in their allowed features. Patients and College users cannot. [Assumption, A3]
- Revenue on the dashboard is shown after discounts, with the total discount alongside. [Assumption, A5]
- The discount can be changed until the visit is closed; who gave it, when and why are stored. [Assumption, A6]
- The medical visit report and partner report data show the discount and payable amount as new fields; existing fields keep their names and meaning. [Assumption, A8]

**Business objective**: Let clinics give patients a discount and keep what is owed, collected and earned consistent everywhere it is shown. [Assumption, A1]

**Ambiguities**
- Which users count as "authorised" (recommendation in A3). (→ Q1)
- Whether revenue counts the amount before or after the discount (recommendation in A5). (→ Q4)

**Missing requirements**
- Discount type and limits (recommendation in A4 and A9). (→ Q2)
- Editing after payment and audit trail (recommendation in A6). (→ Q6)
- Whether reports and the partner API show the discount (recommendation in A8). (→ Q10)
