Executive summary

Adding a discount changes how a visit's fee is stored and how the amount owed and earned is worked out, and that number is read by billing history, the patient portal, the dashboard and every generated report.

Allow authorised users to apply discounts to patient invoices before collecting payment. The final payable amount must be recalculated and reflected in payment records, patient billing history and revenue reports.

Proposed behaviour

  • Store the discount as a new whole-number amount per visit on medical_report. fee_charged keeps its meaning (price before discount). Payable = fee_charged - discount; still owed = payable - fee_paid. Assumption
  • The patient's doctor may always give a discount; any other user only when the NT admin grants a new "Give discount" feature in their allowed features. Patients and College users cannot. Assumption
  • Revenue on the dashboard is shown after discounts, with the total discount alongside. Assumption
  • The discount can be changed until the visit is closed; who gave it, when and why are stored. Assumption
  • The medical visit report and partner report data show the discount and payable amount as new fields; existing fields keep their names and meaning. Assumption

Business objective

Let clinics give patients a discount and keep what is owed, collected and earned consistent everywhere it is shown. Assumption
